# Built-in patterns and hidden events in the synthetic sales

The sales history is generated by `sales/generate.py`. It is synthetic: every rate below is an
assumption chosen to look plausible. This file lists what was built in, so the analytics
agent's answers can be checked. The agent never sees this file.

## Patterns

| Pattern | How it is generated |
|---|---|
| Growth | Daily orders grow about 2% a month from January 2025 |
| Weekends | Friday +10%, Saturday and Sunday +30% |
| Festive season | 1 October to 10 November: orders x2, 10-20% discounts, more bedding, curtains, decor, lighting |
| New Year sale | 1-7 January: orders x1.4, 10% discount |
| Monsoon | July and August: orders x0.85, outdoor items x0.5 |
| Spring | March to May: outdoor items x2 |
| App share | App orders rise from 35% to at most 65% of orders |
| Cancellations | About 3% of orders |
| Returns | 4-8% depending on category; furniture is mostly "damaged in transit", textiles mostly "colour differs from photo" |

## Hidden events

1. **Rug returns, one brand.** From 1 June 2026, rugs from the brand with the most rugs in the
   catalog are returned about 27% of the time (about 8% before), 80% of them with the reason
   "colour differs from photo". The brand is recorded in `data/sales/facts.json`.
2. **Desk sales fall.** From 1 January 2026, desks get 40% fewer order lines than they would
   otherwise.
